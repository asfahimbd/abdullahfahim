The **Bangladesh Red Crescent Society (BDRCS) Chattogram Unit**, located at Enayet Bazar in Chattogram, functions as a critical humanitarian life-line in the region by actively managing local disaster response, community resilience programs, and youth development initiatives. Under the guidance of its local leadership, the unit empowers a large network of youth volunteers to conduct essential first-aid training, manage emergency blood donation drives, and execute rapid relief operations during regional crises like floods and landslides.
