[IEEEXtreme 19.0](https://ieeextreme.org/) was the 19th annual, global 24-hour virtual competitive programming competition organized by the IEEE for its student and graduate student members.

Key Details

- **Date:** Held on October 25, 2025 (starting at 00:00 UTC).
- **Format:** A non-stop, 24-hour coding marathon where participants work in teams of up to three members (proctored by an IEEE member) to solve a series of complex algorithmic and computational challenges.
- **Purpose:** To test participants' technical endurance, coding efficiency, and collaborative problem-solving skills against peers worldwide.
