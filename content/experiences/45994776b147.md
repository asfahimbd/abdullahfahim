      Had an unforgettable experience volunteering at the 27th International Conference on Computer and Information Technology (IEEE ICCIT 2024) held from December 20-22 at Hotel Long Beach, Cox's Bazar, organized by IEEE Bangladesh Section. From organizing paperwork and ensuring smooth presentations to reporting and solving technical issues, it was a challenging yet deeply rewarding journey.  

      This role helped me develop invaluable skills like resilience, perseverance, time management, hosting, problem-solving, and effective teamwork. It was also a wonderful opportunity to connect with volunteers from various universities, authors, professors, researchers, and the IEEE Bangladesh Section community.
