As part of our course Energy Management and Efficiency conducted by Dr. Engr. [**Imran Khan, PhD**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ikr420/) sir, we had the opportunity to visit Bismillah Foundry & Light Casting Industries Ltd., associated with Bright PET Washing Lines Ltd., located at Rajarhat Chuknagar Road, Jashore — along with my team members Shakil Hossain, Nure Modina Sathi and Irin on 11th May, 2025.

This export-based factory manufactures hand pumps, tubewells, vehicle drums, PET flakes, and more. Our objective was to analyze their electrical energy usage, lighting, HVAC systems, overall production process, waste management system and so on.

We identified several issues, including a low power factor despite having a PFI system installed, which we discussed with the proprietor.

A truly insightful day filled with hands-on learning and real-world industrial experience!
