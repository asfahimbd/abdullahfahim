The Varley loop test is a widely-used technique for locating faults (short circuits or earth faults) in underground cables. It is a reliable and efficient method for pinpointing fault locations from the accessible test end.

I developed a functional Varley loop test kit for my university's Electrical and Electronic Measurement Lab as part of a hands-on project. This experience not only deepened my understanding of fault detection in electrical systems but also enhanced my practical skills in designing and building testing equipment.
