Project Demonstration & Submission on EEE 4102 - Control System Sessional
Conducted by: Shuvo Dev Sir, Lecturer, Dept. of EEE, JUST
Demonstrated and submitted project: Self-Balancing Two-Wheel Robot ✅


This project has been a practical learning on PID control, understanding the dynamics of accelerometer tilt angles and gyroscope angular velocity, all crucial for achieving real-time balance using Arduino Nano and MPU6050 sensor.


After nearly 3 months of continuous effort, testing, debugging, and learning we’re proud to see it all come together though with few issues. 
A big shoutout to my teammates specially Sajit Ghising Tamang and I'm grateful to Shuvo Dev sir for pushing us to work on such projects which will help us in future as well as strengthen our theorical base.
