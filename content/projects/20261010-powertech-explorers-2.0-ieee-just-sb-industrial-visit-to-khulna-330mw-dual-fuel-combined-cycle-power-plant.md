Over the past few days, I was under a lot of stress managing all the arrangements, finalizing the participant list, calculating costs, managing polo T-shirts, arranging transportation, meals, and overseeing every little detail. It was truly a completely different kind of challenge for me.

Throughout the entire process on arranging the visit, communicating with the Chief Engineer, and coordinating everything our respected Advisor of IEEE JUST Student Branch, Dr. Mehedi Hassan (Jewel) Sir was constantly by my side. Despite his extremely busy schedule, he provided full support and even joined us on the visit, which was truly remarkable. Alongside him, the presence of Rabiul Islam Sir and Shuvo Dev Sir has always been an inspiration for all of us.

Special mention to Iftiar Rahman Emon and Saklain Mustak who worked with great sincerity to fulfill their responsibilities. Our executive members also made a significant impact.

IEEE JUST SB is gradually growing, and everyone is working hard. InshaAllah, we will do even greater things in the future.

A big thank you to all participants who maintained excellent punctuality throughout the day.
