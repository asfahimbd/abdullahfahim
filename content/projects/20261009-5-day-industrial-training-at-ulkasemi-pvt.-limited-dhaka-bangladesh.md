Finally completed an intense and insightful 5-day industrial training at [**Ulkasemi**](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ulkasemi/) Pvt. Limited, Dhaka from 19th to 23rd May!

We explored the full journey of chip from analog and digital design to custom layout, design & verification, and PnR, gaining a holistic view of the VLSI industry and its promising growth, especially in Bangladesh.

Grateful to Ulkasemi for the opportunity, guidance, and the chance to visit their workstation. The training concluded with a written + MCQ exam and a certificate ceremony, marking a memorable end.

Also, heartfelt thanks to the Dept. of EEE, JUST for nominating us for this incredible experience, where we trained alongside students from 9 other universities across the country!
