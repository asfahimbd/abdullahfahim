description: |-
  On October 12, 2023, IEEE JUST SB successfully conducted the SPAp event PowerTech Explorers: Journey into Bheramara's 410 MW combined cycle power plant.
  The primary goal is to offer participants a unique and in-depth educational experience by allowing them to explore the Bheramara 410 MW Combined Cycle Power Plant. By providing a guided tour of the power plant facilities, participants engaged in a hands-on learning experience and were able to learn the workings of the power plant. It encourages the exchange of ideas and facilitates connections between industry professionals, academics, and students, fostering a collaborative environment in energy technology.
