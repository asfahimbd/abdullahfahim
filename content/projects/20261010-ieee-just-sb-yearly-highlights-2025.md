description: As the Chair of the IEEE JUST Student Branch, I represented the university at the prestigious IEEE BDS SB Chairs' & Counselors' Summit 2025 held in Dhaka, delivering a comprehensive presentation on our "Yearly Highlights 2024 to 2025". My presentation showcased a monumental year of growth, highlighting a record-breaking 264% increase in overall membership, which included scaling to 51 International Members and successfully activating the SPS Chapter and WIE Affinity Group. Furthermore, I detailed our operational success in orchestrating 24 high-impact events—comprising 9 technical sessions, 7 professional seminars, and 4 collaborative initiatives—effectively balancing both online and offline platforms to maximize student engagement and networking.   In addition to celebrating our milestones, I presented strategic solutions implemented during my tenure to overcome skill gaps and financial challenges, such as introducing a dynamic 'Leaderboard System', securing IEEE SPAX funding, and launching the 'Skill Development Expedition 1.0'. The session also emphasized our success in connecting students with distinguished global speakers and industry experts from organizations like Intel and Luleå University of Technology. Concluding the presentation, I outlined a strategic roadmap for the branch's future, setting ambitious targets that include launching the IEEE Power & Energy Society (PES) chapter, organizing a national hackathon, and collaborating on an international IEEE conference with the Bangladesh Section.
