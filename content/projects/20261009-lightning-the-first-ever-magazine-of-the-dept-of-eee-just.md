I am immensely proud to have designed "LIGHTNING", the first-ever magazine of the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE). This was an incredible journey of creativity, learning, and collaboration, marking a historic milestone for our department.

A heartfelt thanks to our Editorial Board (Dr. Mehedi Hassan Jewel sir, Shuvo Dev sir), especially Md. Tarequzzaman Sir, whose unwavering support and encouragement inspired me throughout this project. Your guidance has been invaluable.

This project taught me the importance of teamwork and dedication, and I'm honored to have been part of such a groundbreaking initiative.
