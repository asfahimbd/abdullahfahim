In this session, I covered:

✅ The difference between a good and bad design

✅Why design is essential in IEEE volunteering, research, academics, and freelancing

✅ How to find effective color combinations

✅ Practical tips on starting your first design and what to consider while designing

The goal was simple help participants see the power of design, find motivation, and take their first step toward creativity.

Grateful for the enthusiastic participation and interactive discussions. Looking forward to many more impactful sessions ahead!
